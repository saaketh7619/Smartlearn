Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Visit the login page and click "Forgot Password." You'll receive a reset link within minutes.

Can teachers create custom tests?

Yes — the Teacher Portal provides a full test builder with adaptive difficulty settings and anti-cheat controls.

Is student data shared with third parties?

Never. SmartLearn does not sell or share student data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Does SmartLearn work offline?

Core reading and review content is cached. Network-dependent features like AI Tutor gracefully fall back to offline heuristic mode.
